A note from Kyle

Why I Built VaultPass

When I was dealing with repeated eBay counterfeit removals, I kept finding the same advice online: appeal, wait, sell safer items, and hope things improve.

Some of that advice helped, but it never fully answered what I wanted to know — what happens when I’m ready to return to selling legitimate branded inventory?

That’s why I built VaultPass. I wanted to put the full process, practical examples, and resources together in one place instead of leaving sellers to piece everything together themselves.

— Kyle, creator of Vaultify

Before you join

Questions Before Joining

Is VaultPass a subscription?

No. VaultPass is a one-time $49 purchase with permanent access.

What’s different from the free training?

The free training explains the foundation. VaultPass adds photo examples, listing frameworks, rebuilding guidance, and downloadable checklists and templates.

Does VaultPass guarantee that eBay won’t remove my listings again?

No. Enforcement decisions are controlled by eBay. VaultPass provides educational guidance for legitimate sellers, not guaranteed outcomes.

Is this for replica or counterfeit products?

No. Vaultify is intended only for sellers dealing in legitimate, authentic inventory.

Do I need Vaultify Pro?

No. VaultPass includes the complete paid course. Pro is an optional support membership. Your included 60-day beta access ends without an automatic charge, and your VaultPass access stays permanent.

Can I use VaultPass if my account is currently restricted?

The training can help members understand rebuilding and applicable account considerations, but they must follow eBay’s restrictions and policies. It does not authorize listing while prohibited or bypassing a restriction.
